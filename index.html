About the Model

What is Hesa Adda?°

A chain of modular prefab hubs at national highway intersections, serving truckers, travellers and nearby villages. Each Adda also works as a micro-warehouse for SAAMAAN's local inventory, bringing wholesale supply and essential commerce directly to rural highway corridors.

01 / The Problem

The problem we solve°

Highway Dead Zones

Highway junctions are dead zones with no organised retail or hygienic facilities.

Isolated Villages

Villages within 20 km lack modern commerce access and quick supply availability.

Neglected Truckers

Truckers have no reliable, clean service points, tyre maintenance, or rest zones.

No Rural Service Face

Government services (Aadhaar, ration, insurance) have no rural face on highways.

03 / Franchise Partnership

How the franchise works°

A clear, transparent division of responsibilities with guaranteed operational backing.

Hesa provides:

  • ✓ Prefab modular unit (300-500 sq ft)
  • ✓ Full highway brand kit and roadside signage
  • ✓ Integrated cloud POS tech platform
  • ✓ SAAMAAN wholesale supply chain access
  • ✓ In-depth onboarding and operations training

You (the Hesaathi) provide:

  • ✓ Your land (own, leased or panchayat allotment)
  • ✓ Day-to-day operations and customer service
  • ✓ 2-3 locally hired support staff

Revenue Model: Hesa earns a 20% royalty on gross revenue; you retain 80% of net operational profits.

Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions°

Who can apply? +

Any Indian citizen residing near a national highway intersection who has suitable land can apply to become a Hesaathi entrepreneur.

Do I need my own land? +

No, you do not need to own the land outright. You can apply with self-owned land, leased land (with at least a 3-year registered agreement), or land allocated via a Gram Panchayat NOC.

How much does it cost? +

The set-up cost is about ₹12-18 lakh (projected), covering the prefab modular structure, civil foundation, electricals, tech hardware, and initial SAAMAAN retail inventory.

How long does approval take? +

We guarantee a verification decision within 48 hours of your complete application submission. A local Hesa Manager visits your site in person during this period.

What happens if I am rejected? +

If your land does not meet highway setback or visibility criteria, we share the exact reason transparently. You can immediately apply again with another parcel of land.
